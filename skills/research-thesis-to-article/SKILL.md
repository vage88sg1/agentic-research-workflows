# Thesis to article

## Intake and source freeze

Ask progressively for the thesis/source version, proposed central question, article type, target venue/language, word limits and available results/code. Read the thesis and referenced outputs actually provided. Preserve a frozen source and create a separate article run. Verify current official venue requirements when a venue is selected; use an explicitly generic profile otherwise.

Inventory previous publications/preprints and thesis dissemination supplied by the author. Identify overlap to discuss with the author and venue; never assume a public thesis forbids or guarantees publication. Preserve authorship, permission and declaration questions as unresolved until answered. Do not invent consent, novelty or additional analyses.

## Scope and transformation

Use `assets/conversion-map.csv`: each retained, condensed, corrected, moved-to-supplement or omitted section/result has a thesis locator, destination, reason and affected claim IDs. Agree a coherent question and word budget before rewriting. Shortening must retain essential methods, negative findings, denominators, uncertainty and limitations. Distinguish a focused article from selectively reporting favorable findings or unjustified fragmentation of one study.

A scope editor proposes the narrative and section budget; an evidence mapper records source links; a writer adapts prose for the intended readership; an independent consistency verifier compares key claims, numbers and exclusions against the frozen thesis. Prefer balanced models for writing/mapping, economical for mechanical counts, higher reasoning for central interpretive disputes. Use the installed scientific-writing and citation-management guidance where available; record fallback when unavailable.

## Review and revision

Create an outline and a first conversion with section word counts, then audit claims/result denominators, methods completeness, figure reuse rights and citation currency. New analysis or a materially stronger claim returns to methodological/evidence review before inclusion. Record why a reviewer-requested change is made or declined. Correct underlying records before text; default maximum three passes, stopping after two without substantive progress or missing essential evidence.

Apply [objective, contribution and bibliography completeness](../galileo/references/content-completeness.md) to the selected article scope. Update relevant literature through actual consulted sources when coverage/currency requires it; do not use the thesis bibliography as automatic support. A changed question reopens the conversion map, methods/results interpretation and conclusions. Preserve original versus newly performed contributions.

Deliver article draft, conversion/omission map, word-count report, retained supplements, unresolved declarations and [saved state](references/run-state.md). Use research-project if installed for dependency tracking and research-typesetting for the chosen DOCX/LaTeX route. Offer research-review or research-submission as separate next steps. Conversion itself does not imply acceptance, completed submission or new human approval.
