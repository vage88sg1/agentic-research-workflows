# Systematic review records

Use an explicit protocol covering question/framework, review type, eligibility, information sources, reproducible strategy, search updates, screening and conflict resolution, extraction, design-specific appraisal, synthesis and amendments. Record actual team roles and whether assistance is AI or human. Registration is a separate real-world act; never manufacture a registration status.

CSV assets are starter columns. They record actions actually performed; empty columns are unresolved, not implicit approvals. Extend to preserve database record IDs, report/study linkage, search branches, language/translation and design-specific outcomes. Keep raw exports unchanged. Search record counts, screened records, retrieved reports and included studies are different units; do not assume they are interchangeable or force them into a single total.

Each screening row identifies the stage and report/record, first and second decisions, rationale, reviewer type, conflict and actual adjudication. Keep full-text reports not retrieved separate from full-text exclusion. The extraction file records source locations, outcome/time point, uncertainty and actual second-check status. The appraisal file records the chosen tool/domain, judgment, rationale and verifier; it is not a universal numeric quality score.

For a flow diagram, derive the branch counts from ledgers, confirm that removals and transitions reconcile, and leave counts pending when records are incomplete. Use the appropriate current template; select an extension for other review designs where relevant. PRISMA resources are reporting tools, not a claim that a review met methodological requirements.

Primary starting points, checked 2026-09-27; recheck applicability/current versions for the actual project:

- [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020): reporting checklist and flow-diagram resources.
- [Cochrane Handbook, searching and selecting studies](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04): methodological guidance centered on intervention reviews. Adapt source coverage to the question rather than applying its discipline-specific database recommendations universally.

Do not reproduce copyrighted checklists or claim official endorsement. Link to official current resources and record where each applicable item is addressed in the manuscript.
