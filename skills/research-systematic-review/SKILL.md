# Systematic review workflow

## Scope and protocol

Resolve question/framework appropriate to the discipline, review type, eligibility criteria, outcomes, search sources, date/language restrictions with reasons, screening roles and authorized tools. Use installed literature-review, citation-management, scientific-critical-thinking and statistical-analysis guidance conditionally. Do not adopt a biomedical intervention framework for every question.

Create the protocol using [protocol and record conventions](references/systematic-records.md) before screening decisions or outcome-based synthesis. Record amendments with date/reason and what had already been examined. A protocol written now is not retrospective preregistration. Record real registration only if independently supplied/verified; never register or invent a registration ID as a side effect.

## Search and deduplication

An information specialist develops a search strategy and tests recall against relevant known records where available. Verify current official methodological/reporting guidance appropriate to review type. Save exact database/platform queries, filters, dates, pagination, counts and raw exports. Subject coverage and access limitations govern source selection; one convenient MCP is not comprehensive coverage. Record other methods such as citation searching separately. Consult an experienced librarian where available.

Assign stable record IDs to search hits, report IDs to documents and study IDs to underlying studies. Deduplicate with a retained audit trail; do not merge distinct reports merely because they describe the same study. Keep uncertain duplicates for adjudication. Multiple reports of one study must not be counted as independent participants or effects.

When this step needs external literature, identifier checks or an authorized reference library, follow [literature connection recommendations](../galileo/references/literature-connections.md). Offer relevant MCP connections if equivalent tools are unavailable; reuse the coordinator's recorded choice. Do not propose setup for a task fully supported by supplied sources.

## Screening and extraction

Two screeners work independently with explicit criteria and blinded first-pass decisions where the host supports it. Record reviewer identity/type (human or AI), context separation and stage for each decision. AI roles do not count as two human reviewers. Pilot the criteria, retain disagreements and route conflicts to an adjudicator/responsible user. Without a second independent context, declare the limitation and do not label screening independent.

Screen titles/abstracts then obtain full reports for eligibility assessment. Full-text unavailable is a retrieval status, not evidence of ineligibility. Record explicit full-text exclusion reasons and retain unresolved studies. Never fabricate a screening decision for an unread record. Use the CSV assets for searches, screening and extraction; extend columns to the design.

Extract actual source locators, design/population, intervention/exposure, outcomes/time points, n, estimates/uncertainty, missing data, funding and conflicts where available. Verify extraction through a second check proportionate to the protocol. Choose an appropriate risk-of-bias/appraisal instrument from official current guidance, record domain judgments and rationale, and avoid inventing an overall numeric quality score.

## Synthesis, reporting and correction

The methodologist decides whether studies/estimands/designs and data permit quantitative synthesis. Meta-analysis is optional, not the default output. Predefine treatment of dependence, multiple effects, heterogeneity, missing results and sensitivity analyses. Execute and retain code and actual diagnostics before reporting numerical synthesis. Do not select methods to obtain significance or describe no pooled result as review failure. Assess evidence certainty only with an appropriate framework and recorded judgments.

Generate selection counts from the actual deduplication/retrieval/screening ledgers, keeping records, reports and studies distinct and distinguishing database versus other-source branches. Reconcile counts against ledgers before filling an appropriate PRISMA/extension flow diagram; do not insert illustrative numbers as completed screening. PRISMA is a reporting guideline, not proof of complete searches, sound methods or registration.

A balanced coordinator manages scope and records; information specialist and two screeners use scoped balanced contexts; a higher-reasoning methodologist/appraisal verifier addresses substantive validity; an economical records assistant handles counts. Cost reduction limits redundant context, never silently drops required screening or full-text checks. Pause at agreed search/screening budgets and report incomplete coverage. Scientific correction loops default to three rounds, two without progress; these limits do not authorize abandoning unprocessed eligible records and claiming completion.

Deliver protocol/amendments, search exports/logs, deduplication links, screening decisions/conflicts, extraction/appraisal tables, synthesis/code where executed, reconciled flow counts, reporting-checklist mapping, open issues and [saved state](references/run-state.md). Missing stages remain explicitly incomplete. Use research-drafting/review/typesetting for subsequent writing, simulation and formatting when requested; no real registration, contact, submission or publication occurs automatically.
