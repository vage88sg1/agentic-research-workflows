# Research defense and scientific Q&A

## Intake

Ask about audience/committee specialties, language, talk and Q&A duration, manuscript/slide version and questions the presenter finds difficult. Resolve whether this is coaching (feedback after each answer) or mock examination (feedback after a question block). Use only supplied/consulted research evidence. Do not invent identities or credentials of actual committee members; roles are explicitly simulated.

## Preparation

Read verified claims and limitations. Prepare questions covering motivation, design, measurement, analysis, interpretation, alternative explanations and future work. Adapt to the actual discipline, avoiding clinical/statistical assumptions in unrelated fields. Use `assets/question-bank.csv` for question, rationale, source locator, expected evidence, uncertainty and follow-up. Prepare concise and extended answer outlines and a list of justified backup slides, preserving unanswered questions.

A balanced coordinator plans coverage; domain and methods examiners use separate contexts when supported; an economical timing/copy coach records delivery observations. Escalate only a specific central scientific uncertainty. Examiners receive the same frozen materials without a desired verdict. Do not turn this into simulated journal acceptance or claim a real examination score.

## Interactive rehearsal

Ask one question at a time by default. Wait for the user's answer; do not manufacture their response and grade it as observed performance. In demonstration mode, clearly label both model-generated question and example answer and make no claim about the presenter's ability. During coaching, give actionable feedback on correctness, evidence, clarity and handling uncertainty; suggest a revision and an appropriate follow-up. Permit an honest 'not assessed' or 'we cannot infer that' answer where supported.

Measure elapsed response time only when actually observed; distinguish speaking-time estimates from measured performance and account for pauses/interruption. In mock mode hold coaching until the agreed block ends. Keep criteria transparent and qualitative unless the user supplied a scoring rubric. Record each actual answer and feedback only within the authorized processing boundary.

## Correction and handoff

A substantive error returns to the research evidence/analysis records; wording practice alone cannot repair it. Track improvement across at most three rounds per topic by default and stop after two without progress, missing evidence or the user's chosen stopping point. Deliver question/answer notes, observed versus estimated timing, open scientific issues, and a backup-slide brief. Use research-presentations if installed and explicitly requested to build those slides. Save [run state](references/run-state.md), including the next unanswered question, so resuming does not invent previous performance.
